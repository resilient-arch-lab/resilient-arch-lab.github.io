Lab Director, Resilient Architecture (ResArch) Lab

Dr. Dean Sullivan

Assistant Professor · Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering · University of New Hampshire

Dr. Sullivan is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of New Hampshire, where he leads the Resilient Architecture (ResArch) Lab. His research focuses on systems software and architectural security, side-channel attacks and defenses, and hardware security. He earned his Ph.D. in Computer Engineering from the University of Florida in 2021 under the guidance of Dr. Yier Jin, and his B.S. in Electrical Engineering and M.S. in Computer Engineering from the University of Central Florida.

His office is in Kingsbury Hall W213, 33 Academic Way, Durham, NH 03824. He can be reached at dean.sullivan@unh.edu.
